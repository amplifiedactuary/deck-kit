## The experiment

A test of the *iceberg* device: take the most familiar metaphor in business and see
whether production-grade execution can rescue the cliché. The scene is hand-rolled
SVG art direction — no clip-art: a faceted crystalline iceberg with a luminous
waterline, an underwater depth gradient that darkens toward the seabed, angled
light rays, and slow-drifting marine particles. Above the waterline sits the small
visible tip (the customer experience — what policyholders see, ~15% of the mass).
Below it, the vast submerged body is divided into four labelled strata that
illuminate in pairs as the steps advance: pricing & underwriting, claims operations,
data & reporting, compliance & governance. A depth-gauge bracket then quantifies
the ratio: 4 layers of operations : 1 customer experience.

The wager being tested: the metaphor is tired, but *craft* (real scene lighting,
restrained palette, purposeful step choreography) may make it land anyway for a
senior non-coder audience.

## Steps

| Step | Reveals |
|------|---------|
| 0 | Ocean scene: sky, glowing waterline, the visible tip + "THE CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE" callout. Submerged mass is a barely-visible silhouette. |
| 1 | Strata 01–02 illuminate with labels + annotations: *pricing & underwriting*, *claims operations*. |
| 2 | Strata 03–04 illuminate: *data & reporting*, *compliance & governance*. |
| 3 | Depth-gauge brackets ("1 CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE" above water, "4 LAYERS OF OPERATIONS" below) + centred ratio line and closing statement. |

**SafeMode:** the full scene fully lit and static (all strata, gauge, closing line;
no ray shimmer, no particle drift). This is also the overview poster state.
